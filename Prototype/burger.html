Burger

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef patty
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cheese slices
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato (sliced)
  • Onion (sliced)
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise
  • Cooking oil or butter

Steps

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and pepper, then form into patties.
  2. Heat a pan or grill and lightly oil it.
  3. Cook the patties for 3–5 minutes on each side until fully cooked.
  4. Place cheese on top of the patty and let it melt.
  5. Toast the burger buns lightly on the pan.
  6. Spread ketchup and mayonnaise on the buns.
  7. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and the cooked patty.
  8. Cover with the top bun and serve hot.
