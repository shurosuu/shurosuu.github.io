Fried Chicken

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti noodles
  • Ground beef or pork
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Onion (chopped)
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Sugar (optional)
  • Italian seasoning or oregano (optional)
  • Grated cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot and add salt.
  2. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to the package instructions, then drain.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  4. Add ground meat and cook until browned.
  5. Pour in tomato sauce and tomato paste, then stir well.
  6. Add salt, pepper, sugar, and seasonings. Simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  7. Place spaghetti on a plate and pour the sauce on top.
  8. Add grated cheese if desired and serve hot.
