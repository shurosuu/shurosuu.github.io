Pancakes

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Egg
  • Melted butter or cooking oil
  • Vanilla extract (optional)
  • Syrup or honey (for serving)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Add milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Whisk until the batter is smooth.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour some batter onto the pan to form a circle.
  6. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook the other side.
  7. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  8. Serve warm with syrup or your favorite toppings.
