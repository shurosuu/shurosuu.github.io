Spaghetti

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Onion (chopped)
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Sugar
  • Grated cheese

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
  2. Add spaghetti noodles and cook until tender. Drain and set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic and onion.
  4. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Pour in tomato sauce and tomato paste, then stir well.
  6. Season with salt, pepper, and sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Mix the sauce with the cooked spaghetti or pour sauce on top.
  8. Sprinkle grated cheese and serve hot.
