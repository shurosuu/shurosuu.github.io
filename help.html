Help Center

Welcome to the RecipeBox Help Center. Here you can learn how to use RecipeBox, read tutorials, find answers to common questions, and discover helpful tips.

Getting Started

Step 1: Open RecipeBox and browse the available recipe categories.
Step 2: Use the search bar to quickly find a recipe.
Step 3: Click a recipe card to view its ingredients and cooking instructions.
Step 4: Click the Create button to add your own recipes.

Tutorial

🔍 Search Recipes
Type the name of a recipe in the search bar and press the search button.
🍴 View Recipes
Click any recipe card to see the complete recipe information.
📂 Browse Categories
Scroll through Popular, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Dessert, and Drinks.
➕ Create Recipes
Use the Create button to add your own recipes to RecipeBox.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I search for a recipe?
Type the recipe name into the search bar and click the search button.
How do I open a recipe?
Click on any recipe card to view its ingredients and cooking instructions.
How do I add my own recipe?
Click the Create button and enter the recipe information.
Can I save favorite recipes?
Yes. You can save recipes to your Favorites for easy access.
What recipe categories are available?
RecipeBox includes Popular Recipes, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Dessert, and Drinks.
Why can't I find a recipe?
Make sure the recipe name is spelled correctly or browse the available categories.
Do I need an internet connection?
If RecipeBox is connected to an online database, internet access may be required.
Who can use RecipeBox?
RecipeBox is designed for beginners and experienced home cooks alike.
