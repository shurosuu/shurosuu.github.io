Matcha Latte

Ingredients

  • Matcha powder
  • Hot water
  • Milk (fresh or any milk of choice)
  • Sugar or honey
  • Ice cubes (optional, for iced matcha latte)

Steps

  1. Sift 1–2 teaspoons of matcha powder into a cup to remove clumps.
  2. Add a small amount of hot water and whisk until smooth and frothy.
  3. Heat the milk in a pan or microwave.
  4. Add sugar or honey to the milk and stir well.
  5. Pour the warm milk into the matcha mixture and stir gently.
  6. For iced matcha latte, add ice cubes before pouring the milk.
  7. Serve and enjoy.
